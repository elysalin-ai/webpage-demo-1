Questions

Are you actually in Columbus?

The studio is in Dublin, Ohio — part of the greater Columbus area, northwest of downtown. Google Maps: 5910 Venture Drive, Suite A, Dublin, OH 43017. We write Columbus because most guests live or work in the Columbus metro and search that way.

Why not book a spa closer to downtown?

If you are already downtown and need something in walking distance, choose a closer studio. If you can drive 20 minutes for a $70 hour, quieter rooms, and evening availability, we are built for that.

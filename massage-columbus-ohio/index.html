Signature Classic — Swedish style

Long, even strokes and steady pressure that help the whole body soften. The hour moves through the back, shoulders, and legs with a calm rhythm — made to leave you lighter, quieter, and reset.

A slower session with firmer, concentrated pressure on areas of muscular tightness — commonly the neck, shoulders, upper back, and hips. The pace is measured so those regions can release, rather than only being treated with light, passing strokes.

Choose Mediterranean massage when you prefer deeper, targeted work instead of a full-body Swedish flow.

How close we are

Sawmill and Bridge Park are typically 10–15 minutes. Downtown Columbus and the Short North are usually 20–25 minutes off-peak. We are not a downtown storefront — that is the point: later hours, posted prices, and a still room.

Before you book

Is this a one-person room or can we come together?

A standard visit is reserved for one guest in a private room. If you would like to share the hour, please book a couples massage so two tables can be prepared in one suite. For a different arrangement, kindly text or call (614) 815-3399 and we will be happy to check availability.

What should I wear, and how early should I arrive?

Please wear whatever feels comfortable to travel in. Arriving a few minutes early helps check-in stay relaxed. When you arrive, you are welcome to tell us your preferred pressure, any areas that feel tender, and whether you would prefer little or no scent.

I am expecting — can I still come in?

Please let us know you are expecting, and how far along you are, when you book. We will confirm whether we can welcome you that week and how we can make the table comfortable. If your physician has advised you to postpone bodywork, we kindly ask that you follow that guidance.

Are essential oils used during the massage?

Swedish Classic and Mediterranean sessions use unscented or very lightly scented oil unless you request otherwise. If you would like a custom essential-oil blend, please book aromatherapy and we will choose it with you at the start of the visit.

I have a sore joint, a bruise, or I just had a procedure.

Please mention any injury, bruising, or recent procedure when you check in. We will gladly avoid that area or work gently around it. Our sessions are intended for relaxation and comfort, and are not a substitute for medical care.

Can I send this through insurance?

We are not able to bill insurance directly. After your visit, we are happy to email a paid receipt if you would like one for your records.

What if my plans change?

Please refer to the cancellation and reschedule terms shown in Vagaro when you book. If your plans change, we appreciate a call or text to (614) 815-3399 as soon as you are able so we can offer the time to another guest.

I searched Columbus — where do I actually drive?

We are located at 5910 Venture Drive, Suite A, Dublin, OH 43017 — in northwest Columbus, a short drive from Sawmill Road, Bridge Park, Powell, and Worthington. Parking is available at the building.

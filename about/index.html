Our Story

Wellness is a return.

Palm Haven was born from a simple belief: that wellness is not a luxury, but a return. A return to breath. To touch. To the parts of ourselves we have quietly set aside.

Every treatment is shaped by tropical traditions and refined through care for each guest who walks through our doors on Venture Drive. Our therapists are masters of their craft — and listeners.

We opened in Dublin so northwest Columbus would have a still, green room for Classic massage, aromatherapy, and couples sessions. Hours run daily 10am–9pm so care can happen after work, not only on weekends.

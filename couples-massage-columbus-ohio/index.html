Couples Massage · Columbus, Ohio

A reserved hour, shared

Come in from the city and let the room go still. Palm Haven sits among the quieter streets of Dublin, on the northwest edge of Columbus — a green studio kept for rest, not display. Here the two of you are given the same hour. The bodywork is unhurried. The air stays calm. You leave the visit the way you entered it: together.

The shared session

A couples session is the same careful bodywork we offer one guest — only the hour is shared. Signature Classic, Mediterranean, or Aromatherapy can be chosen independently, so each body receives the pace it needs.

Nothing in the treatment is hurried. Shoulders, back, and the places that stay tight are given time. The setting remains a small studio, quiet and green, on the edge of Columbus.

If you would like the session extended, ten minutes of head, foot, warm stone, or face work may be added for $15 per guest.

What the body takes from the hour

Tension gathers where the week has been held — along the neck, through the shoulders, down the back. Side by side, that holding can be released at the same pace. Circulation returns. The breath lengthens. The room does the rest: low light, still air, and enough quiet for the nervous system to stand down.

There is also the simpler gift of not being separated. One guest does not wait in another room while the other is cared for. The visit has one beginning and one end.

People often describe the hour afterward as clearer. The body is softer. The mind is less crowded. That is the point of coming here together.

For a date, a milestone, or no reason at all

Some reservations mark an anniversary or a birthday. Some are simply an evening stolen back from the week — a place to set the city down and sit in a greener quiet.

The room is equally suited to partners, to close friends, and to family who do not often get an hour without interruption.

Frequently asked questions

Can we book two separate rooms instead of one shared room?

Yes, when two individual rooms are available at the same time. Tell us when you book, or text (614) 815-3399, and we will confirm the schedule. If a second room is not free, we will offer the next time that works.

Is the couples room private?

Yes. The room is reserved for your appointment only. No other guests share it.

Can each of us choose a different style?

Yes. One guest may book Signature Classic and the other Mediterranean or Aromatherapy. Note the preference when you reserve.

Can the pressure be different on each table?

Yes. Each session is adjusted separately. Share injuries, sensitive areas, and how much pressure you want at the start.

May we add head, foot, warm stone, or face work?

Yes. Ten minutes may be added for $15 per guest. Ask when you book or at check-in.

Do prepaid packages apply?

Individual prepaid packages do not apply to a couples reservation.

What should we bring?

Wear clothing that is easy to change. Arrive a few minutes early. Tell us about pregnancy, recent injury, or scent sensitivity before the session begins.

How do we book?

Reserve on Vagaro or call (614) 815-3399. Ask for a couples appointment and the length you want — 60 or 90 minutes.

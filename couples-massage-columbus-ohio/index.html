Couples Massage in Columbus, Ohio

Two therapists. One private suite. A quiet hour for Columbus date nights, anniversaries, and friends.

Couples massage at Palm Haven is built for Columbus guests who want to unplug together without a hotel-spa bill. You settle side by side while two therapists work in sync. The studio is in Dublin — northwest Columbus — with parking at the door and hours until 9pm.

Classic or aromatherapy

Classic is firm, familiar bodywork for both of you. Aromatherapy adds organic essential oil blends. Either session can be 60 or 90 minutes. Prepaid packages do not apply to couples bookings.

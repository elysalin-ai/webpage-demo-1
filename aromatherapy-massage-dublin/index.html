How does aromatherapy work?

The blend is chosen with you at the start — grounding, floral, or a brighter citrus — then worked into the same full-body sequence as our Classic hour. Scent meets the breath first. Touch follows.

The oil is not a perfume poured over the room. It is a small amount, selected so the nervous system can settle rather than be crowded. If a note does not agree with you, we change it before the session begins.

What should you expect?

You arrive a few minutes early. We ask about pressure, tender areas, and scent. Then you rest on the table while the hour moves through the back, shoulders, and legs — unhurried, the way a Classic session is built.

The difference is only the blend. The lights stay low. The room stays quiet. Tell us if you prefer little or no scent after all; we will adjust.

Benefits of aromatherapy massage

The body receives the same release as any full session: shoulders drop, the breath lengthens, the week loosens its hold. The blend is there to support that — calm when the day has been sharp, lift when the body feels heavy.

It is not a medical treatment. It is an hour in which scent and touch work together so the visit can land more completely.

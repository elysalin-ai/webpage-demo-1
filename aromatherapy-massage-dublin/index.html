Aromatherapy Massage in Dublin

Organic essential oil blends and therapeutic touch at Palm Haven Spa in Dublin, Ohio.

Aromatherapy massage at Palm Haven pairs classic bodywork with oils chosen for the nervous system — calm, ease, or lift. It is still a full massage. The difference is scent, working with your breath from the first minute on the table.

Tell us if you prefer grounding, floral, or brighter citrus notes. We keep the room quiet so the blend can do its work. For firmer pressure without custom oils, book our Mediterranean Classic massage.

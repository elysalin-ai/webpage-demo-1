Couples Massage in Dublin, Ohio

Share a private suite at Palm Haven Spa. Two therapists, one quiet hour — Classic or aromatherapy.

Couples massage in Dublin is one of our most requested bookings — anniversaries, date nights, and friends who want to unplug together. You settle side by side in our candlelit suite while two therapists work in sync.

Two ways to book

Mediterranean / Classic is firm, familiar bodywork for both of you. Aromatherapy adds personalized organic essential oil blends. Either can be 60 or 90 minutes. Add-ons (head, foot, hot stone, face) can be added to each table.

Prepaid packages apply only to single-person Classic massage, not couples sessions.

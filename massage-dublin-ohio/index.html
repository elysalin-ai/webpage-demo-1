Mediterranean / Classic Massage

This is our signature individual session. Therapists use timeless classic techniques with Mediterranean-inspired firm pressure to release tension in the back, neck, shoulders, and hips. Tell us where you hold stress; we adjust pressure and focus so the hour is yours.

Choose 60 minutes for a full-body reset, or 90 minutes when you want extra time on stubborn areas. Add 10-minute enhancements for head, feet, hot stone, or face and eye relaxation.

Questions

No. Many guests drive from Columbus and nearby suburbs. We are on Venture Drive in Dublin, a short trip from Sawmill Road and Bridge Park.

Is this a deep tissue massage?

Classic / Mediterranean work uses firm, focused pressure. Tell your therapist if you want lighter or stronger work — every session is adjusted.
